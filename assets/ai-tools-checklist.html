🤖 AI Tools Checklist for Small Business

21 tools organized by use case. Know exactly what to use, what it costs, and whether it's right for your business.

📞 Customer Support

Intercom Fin $0.99/resolution

AI-first support agent. Answers questions from your docs, escalates to humans when needed. Best-in-class AI quality but gets expensive at scale.

Best for: Teams with 100+ tickets/month who want premium AI quality

Tidio Free tier / $29+/mo

Chat widget with AI chatbot. Great for e-commerce. Free tier is generous. AI isn't as smart as Intercom but much cheaper.

Best for: E-commerce stores, solopreneurs, budget-conscious teams

Crisp Free tier / $25+/mo

All-in-one customer messaging with chat, email, and knowledge base. Clean interface, good value.

Best for: Startups wanting one tool for support, marketing, and sales chat

Help Scout $20+/user/mo

Email-first support with AI features. Human-centered approach. Great for teams who prefer email over chat.

Best for: Service businesses, consultancies, B2B with email-heavy support

📈 Sales & Outreach

Clay $149+/mo

Data enrichment and personalized outreach at scale. Connect 50+ data sources, AI-write personalized emails. Powerful but learning curve.

Best for: Sales teams doing high-volume outbound who need personalization

Apollo Free tier / $49+/mo

Lead database + outreach sequences. 270M+ contacts. Free tier gets you started. Good balance of features and price.

Best for: B2B startups, sales reps who need leads + sequences in one tool

Instantly $37+/mo

Cold email at scale with unlimited email accounts. AI warmup, deliverability focused. No lead data—bring your own.

Best for: Cold emailers who have their own lead lists

Lavender Free tier / $29+/mo

AI email coach that helps you write better sales emails. Real-time suggestions. Works inside Gmail/Outlook.

Best for: Individual sales reps who want to improve reply rates

✍️ Content Creation

Jasper $49+/mo

Enterprise-grade AI writing. Templates, brand voice, team collaboration. Powerful but pricey for solopreneurs.

Best for: Marketing teams, agencies creating lots of content

Copy.ai Free tier / $36+/mo

AI copywriting with workflows. Good for social posts, ads, email. Free tier is useful.

Best for: Marketers who need quick copy for multiple channels

Descript Free tier / $15+/mo

Edit video/podcast by editing text transcript. AI removes filler words, eye contact correction, voice cloning.

Best for: Podcasters, YouTubers, anyone doing video content

Canva Free tier / $13+/mo

Design tool with AI features (text-to-image, magic resize, background removal). Easy for non-designers.

Best for: Everyone—social graphics, presentations, basic design

⚙️ Operations & Productivity

Notion AI $10/user/mo addon

AI assistant inside Notion. Summarize, write, brainstorm, Q&A your workspace. Requires Notion subscription.

Best for: Existing Notion users who want AI integrated into their workspace

Fireflies.ai Free tier / $10+/mo

AI meeting transcription and summaries. Joins Zoom/Meet/Teams automatically. Search across all meetings.

Best for: Anyone in lots of meetings who needs searchable records

Zapier Free tier / $19+/mo

Connect apps and automate workflows. Now with AI features for building zaps and data formatting.

Best for: Anyone automating repetitive tasks across multiple tools

Reclaim.ai Free tier / $10+/mo

AI calendar assistant. Auto-schedules habits, tasks, 1:1s. Protects focus time. Syncs with Asana/Todoist.

Best for: Busy people who struggle with calendar management

Otter.ai Free tier / $8+/mo

Meeting transcription with real-time captions. OtterPilot joins meetings automatically. Good free tier.

Best for: Individuals who need affordable meeting transcription

💡 Decision Framework

How to Pick the Right Tool

  1. Start with the problem, not the tool. What specific task takes too much time or money?
  2. Try free tiers first. Most tools have generous free plans. Test before committing.
  3. Calculate ROI. If a $50/mo tool saves 10 hours/month, that's $5/hour—worth it?
  4. Check integrations. Does it connect to your existing tools?
  5. Read recent reviews. AI tools change fast. Reviews from 6+ months ago may be outdated.

📦 Example Stacks

$0/month Stack (Solopreneur Starting Out)

  • Support: Tidio (free tier)
  • Sales: Apollo (free tier)
  • Content: Canva (free) + Copy.ai (free tier)
  • Operations: Fireflies (free tier) + Zapier (free tier)

~$50/month Stack (Growing Business)

  • Support: Crisp ($25/mo)
  • Sales: Lavender ($29/mo, individual)
  • Content: Canva Pro ($13/mo) + Descript ($15/mo)
  • Operations: Reclaim ($10/mo) + Fireflies ($10/mo)

~$150/month Stack (Scaling Team)

  • Support: Intercom Fin ($0.99/resolution) or Help Scout ($40/mo)
  • Sales: Apollo Pro ($49/mo) + Clay ($149/mo)
  • Content: Jasper ($49/mo) + Descript ($24/mo)
  • Operations: Notion + AI ($15/mo) + Zapier Pro ($49/mo)
