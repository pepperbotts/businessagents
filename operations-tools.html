1. Notion AI — Best for Knowledge Work

Notion AI adds a layer of intelligence to your Notion workspace. Ask questions about your docs, summarize pages, draft content, brainstorm ideas. It knows your context.

The real talk: If you already use Notion (and you probably should), the AI add-on is a no-brainer. Being able to ask "what did we decide about X?" and get an answer from your docs is genuinely useful. If you don't use Notion, this isn't the reason to start.

✓ Pros

  • Knows your workspace context
  • Q&A across all your docs
  • Writing assistance built-in
  • Natural integration

✗ Cons

  • Requires Notion subscription
  • Additional $10/user
  • Limited to Notion ecosystem
💰 $10/user/mo add-on

2. Fireflies.ai — Best for Meeting Notes

Fireflies joins your meetings, transcribes everything, and gives you summaries with action items. Integrates with Zoom, Meet, Teams, and basically everything else.

The real talk: If you're in lots of meetings and hate taking notes, Fireflies is the move. The free tier gives you 800 minutes/month—plenty to try it. The AI summaries are good enough that you can often skip watching the recording.

✓ Pros

  • Works with all major platforms
  • Automatic summaries + action items
  • Searchable transcripts
  • Good free tier

✗ Cons

  • Some people find bots joining creepy
  • Accuracy varies with audio quality
  • Storage limits on free
💰 Free — $19/mo for more features
Try Fireflies Free →

3. Zapier AI — Best for Automation

Zapier connects 6,000+ apps. The AI features let you build automations by describing what you want in plain English, and use AI to transform data mid-flow.

The real talk: Zapier is the automation standard for a reason—it just works. The AI features make it accessible to non-technical people. The free tier is limited (100 tasks/month) but enough to see if automation helps you.

✓ Pros

  • 6,000+ app integrations
  • Plain English automation building
  • AI data transformation
  • Reliable and mature

✗ Cons

  • Gets expensive at scale
  • Free tier very limited
  • Complex automations need patience
💰 Free — $69/mo for serious use
Try Zapier Free →

4. Reclaim.ai — Best for Scheduling

Reclaim is an AI scheduling assistant that protects your focus time, auto-schedules tasks and habits, and finds meeting times across teams. Calendar Tetris, solved.

The real talk: If you're constantly losing focus time to meetings, Reclaim is worth trying. It automatically defends blocks of time for deep work and reschedules things intelligently. The free tier is genuinely useful.

✓ Pros

  • Protects focus time automatically
  • Smart rescheduling
  • Task scheduling
  • Good free tier

✗ Cons

  • Requires trust in the AI
  • Takes time to learn your patterns
  • Some features need paid plan
💰 Free — $12/mo for teams
Try Reclaim Free →

5. Otter.ai — Best for Transcription

Otter does real-time transcription and meeting summaries. OtterPilot auto-joins your meetings. It's like Fireflies but more focused on the transcription angle.

The real talk: If you need accurate transcription more than meeting summaries, Otter is the pick. Real-time transcription is especially useful for interviews or podcasts. Free tier is decent for light use.

✓ Pros

  • Real-time transcription
  • Good accuracy
  • Collaboration features
  • Works on mobile

✗ Cons

  • Less focused on action items
  • Similar pricing to Fireflies
  • Limited free minutes
💰 Free — $17/mo for more minutes
Try Otter Free →

6. Bardeen — Best for Browser Automation

Bardeen lives in your browser and automates repetitive web tasks. Scrape data, fill forms, control apps—all from natural language commands or pre-built playbooks.

The real talk: Bardeen is a power user tool. If you find yourself doing the same thing in your browser over and over, it can probably automate it. The learning curve is worth it for the right use cases.

✓ Pros

  • Automates browser tasks
  • Natural language commands
  • Pre-built playbooks
  • Generous free tier

✗ Cons

  • Learning curve
  • Browser-based limitations
  • Not for everyone
💰 Free — $10/mo for power features
