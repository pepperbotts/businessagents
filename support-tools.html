1. Intercom Fin — Best for High-Volume Support

Intercom Fin is the AI agent from Intercom, one of the most established names in customer messaging. It's not cheap, but it works. Fin learns from your help docs and past conversations to resolve tickets without human intervention.

The real talk: Intercom claims 50%+ resolution rates, and from what we've seen, that's not BS for businesses with good documentation. But you need existing support volume to justify the cost. If you're getting 10 tickets a week, this isn't for you.

✓ Pros

  • Actually resolves tickets, not just deflects
  • Learns from your specific knowledge base
  • Seamless human handoff when needed
  • Pay per resolution = aligned incentives

✗ Cons

  • Expensive for low-volume businesses
  • Requires good documentation to work well
  • Full Intercom platform can be overwhelming
💰 From $0.99/resolution + Intercom subscription

2. Tidio — Best for E-commerce

Tidio combines live chat with AI chatbots specifically designed for online stores. It integrates with Shopify, WooCommerce, and most e-commerce platforms out of the box.

The real talk: If you run an e-commerce store and want to start with AI support without a huge investment, Tidio is probably your best bet. The free tier is actually usable, and the AI features in paid plans are solid for common questions like "where's my order?" and "what's your return policy?"

✓ Pros

  • Great e-commerce integrations
  • Genuinely useful free tier
  • Easy to set up (under an hour)
  • Affordable paid plans

✗ Cons

  • AI not as sophisticated as Intercom
  • Limited customization on free plan
  • Can feel basic for complex support needs
💰 Free — $29/mo for AI features
Try Tidio Free →

3. Crisp — Best for Startups Scaling Up

Crisp is an all-in-one customer messaging platform with AI-powered features like MagicReply that drafts responses for you. It's free for 2 users, which makes it perfect for early-stage startups.

The real talk: Crisp sits in a nice middle ground—more capable than Tidio, more affordable than Intercom. The AI isn't autonomous (it suggests replies rather than handling tickets alone), but for teams who want to stay in control while moving faster, that's actually a feature.

✓ Pros

  • Free for 2 users
  • AI copilot helps write better responses
  • Built-in CRM and campaign tools
  • Good multichannel support

✗ Cons

  • Jump to $95/mo for AI features
  • AI assists rather than resolves
  • Interface can feel cluttered
💰 Free (2 users) — $95/mo for MagicReply
Try Crisp Free →

4. Help Scout — Best for Human-First Support

Help Scout is a traditional help desk with AI features bolted on thoughtfully. The AI summarizes long threads, suggests replies, and helps draft responses—but humans stay in control.

The real talk: If you believe customer support should feel human (but want AI to make your humans faster), Help Scout is the play. It won't auto-resolve tickets, but it'll cut your response time significantly. Good for businesses where personal touch matters.

✓ Pros

  • Clean, focused interface
  • AI enhances rather than replaces humans
  • Excellent knowledge base tool
  • Great for email-based support

✗ Cons

  • No free tier
  • Per-user pricing adds up
  • Less autonomous than competitors
💰 From $20/user/mo
Try Help Scout →

Which One Should You Choose?

Choose Intercom Fin if: You have high ticket volume, good documentation, and budget for premium tools. The per-resolution pricing actually saves money at scale.

Choose Tidio if: You run an e-commerce store and want affordable AI support that just works with your existing setup.

Choose Crisp if: You're a startup scaling up and want a capable free tier now with room to grow.

Choose Help Scout if: You want AI to augment your human support team, not replace it.
